    Duel controls
    Phone
    Joystick on the left. Punch, Kick, Special, Dodge (Dash for rook and queen) and Grab on the right.
    Keyboard
    WASD or arrows move · F/J punch · G/K kick · H/L special · E/Space dodge or dash · R/U grab (or punch+kick)
    Defence
    Hold back to block highs and mids, down-back to block lows. Dodge (pawn, knight, bishop) slips sideways past a straight attack so you can hit back; rook and queen dash forward or back instead. Grab works up close and goes straight through a block; press Grab the moment you are grabbed to break free.
    Offence
    Every piece has its own move list: strings like punch, punch, kick, a launcher on forward+kick to start juggles, lows on down, and a slow heavy on back+punch. Hitting someone mid-attack is a counter hit (+25%).
    Meter
    Specials cost one of the two SP bars. They fill as you hit, block and get hit. Under 30% HP (and after losing a good chunk of the health you walked in with) you enter rage: hold down + Special for a one-time rage art. Blocking heavy hits still costs a sliver of health.

    House rules
    • Normal chess moves. A capture starts a duel between the two pieces.
    • An unprotected piece is simply taken, with no duel. A piece is protected when another piece of its side could recapture on its square. Only protected pieces get a fight.
    • Backup counts. Each other piece of yours that bears on the square (up to 3) starts your fighter with more SP and makes it hit 12% harder. This works for the attacker and the defender.
    • If the attacker wins, it takes the square and keeps whatever HP it ended with. The loser is removed.
    • If the attacker loses, the defender stays put and the attacker falls back to its own square with 25% health (or what it had, if that was less).
    • The king never fights. A king captures instantly, and nobody can duel a king.
    • At the start of your turn, pieces touching your king heal.
    • When time runs out, the side with the lower share of its own max HP loses. A tie goes to the defender.
    • If you are in check and your capture fails, your king falls and you lose. The game warns you before any duel like that.
    • A pawn that promotes becomes the piece you choose, at the same share of HP.
    • Each player has 10 minutes of thinking time. The clock stops during duels. Run out and you lose.
