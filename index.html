Big moments
Specials cost half your SP meter (two bars under your HP). It fills when you land hits, take hits, or block. Hits during their attack are counters (+30%). Below 30% HP you enter rage (+15% damage) and get one rage art on Special. Throws can't be blocked, press throw right as you're grabbed to break it.
Hold away from your opponent to block. Crouch-block stops low attacks. Double-tap a direction to dash, if your piece can. Up again in the air double-jumps.
Fighting styles
  • Pawn small scrapper. Low hop, no dash. Hits 50% harder below a quarter HP.
  • Knight jumper. Highest jump plus double jump, L-leap dive kick, strong air attacks. No dash.
  • Bishop diagonal zoner. Punch fires 45° bolts, special teleports diagonally. Weak up close.
  • Rook tank. Can't jump. Fast straight dash, armor, floor shockwave, up+punch uppercut.
  • Queen all-rounder. Dashes anywhere, double jump, fast bolt, longest combos.

House rules
  • Normal chess moves. A capture starts a duel between the two pieces.
  • The loser is removed. The winner keeps whatever HP it ended with.
  • If the attacker wins, it takes the square. If it loses, the defender stays put.
  • The king never fights. A king captures instantly, and nobody can duel a king.
  • At the start of your turn, pieces touching your king heal.
  • When time runs out, the side with the lower share of its own max HP loses. A tie goes to the defender.
  • If a failed capture leaves your own king in check, your king falls and you lose. The game warns you before any duel like that.
  • A pawn that promotes becomes a queen at the same share of HP.
